A metronome and a tuner, for practising an instrument.

- Turn the wheel on Home to metronome and tap it. The play key starts and pauses it.
- The wheel sets the tempo, from 40 to 208. You can also drag a finger round the ring, or tap the middle a few times in time.
- The dots light each beat of the bar, and the first beat sounds higher and louder. Tap 4/4 to change the time signature, the note to count in eighths, triplets or sixteenths, and the speaker to pick one of six sounds.
- Under the dial is how long you have played today, also shown on the side wheel. Tap it for the last five weeks, each day lit by how long you played.
- The tuning fork opens the tuner, for guitar, bass or ukulele. Play a string to see its note and how far off it is, in yellow once it is within 3 cents. Tap a string to hold the tuner on it, and tap tuning for drop D, DADGAD and the others.
- The tuner listens only while its page is open.
