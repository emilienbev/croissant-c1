Things to do, with no day or time to them.

- Turn the wheel on Home to reminders and tap it, or hold the side button and ask: "remind me to buy oat milk". Give Ask a day or a time and it goes in the calendar instead.
- The plus key opens a new reminder. Type it on the keys and press done.
- Reminders still to do are listed under the day you added them, the newest first. Ticked ones stay a week, folded under done, which a tap opens.
- The wheel moves between them and a tap opens one. Its check key ticks it done, or puts a done one back. The red X deletes it, after a confirm. Tap the title to rename it.
- A tap on Home shows the newest two under to do, with the rest of your day. Ask can also read them back, tick them off, rename or delete them.
