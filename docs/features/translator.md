Say something in one language, read it in another.

- Hold the side button and talk. Here a hold translates instead of going to Ask. Your words show in white, the translation in green.
- Tap a language to pick another among english, french, german, italian, spanish and japanese. Tap "to" to swap them.
- Every translation is kept. Saved, bottom left, lists them by day. The wheel shows the one in focus in full.
- The R1 key, bottom right, translates on the device once its offline models are installed. Online is better, so keep it for a shaky network.
- The side wheel shows the current pair under translator.
