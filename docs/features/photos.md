Photos you keep, pictures made for you, and a way to get them onto a phone.

- Opens from the wheel on Home, or the photos key on the camera.
- Photos are listed by day, newest first, under generated.
- A photo's page shows when it was taken and what you asked about it. Tap the picture for full screen, or hold the side button to ask about it.
- The transform key, the spark, makes a new picture from the photo. Hold the side button, say what you want, and it lands in generated.
- The send key shows a code. Scan it with a phone on the same wi-fi or tailnet and the photos open there.
- Press and hold to pick several, then send or delete them together. Delete asks first.

Ask makes pictures from words. The server draws them, and they show up in generated about a minute later.

| You say | Ask calls |
|---|---|
| "draw a lighthouse at dusk" | `image_generate {"prompt": "a lighthouse at dusk"}` |
